# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for contributors and users regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while still being clear about technical or security concerns.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal, customer, order, credential, or security-sensitive data.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For private conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer through a private channel rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
