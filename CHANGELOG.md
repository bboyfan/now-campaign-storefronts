# Changelog

All notable changes to WC Campaign are documented here.

## 1.1.1

Initial public / WordPress.org release candidate.

- Campaign pricing for simple products and variations.
- Quick Order, Editorial, and Compact campaign storefront layouts.
- Campaign section builder, media gallery, rich content, and shortcode support.
- WooCommerce cart/session integration and campaign order attribution.
- HPOS-compatible, refund-aware campaign reporting.
- Password-protected live campaign reports with product performance.
- Theme-aware presentation and isolated campaign commerce controls.
- Traditional Chinese (`zh_TW`) localization.
